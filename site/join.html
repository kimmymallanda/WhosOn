WhosOn

🎾 Beta for ALTA captains

Stop sending 50 texts to find out who can play.

WhosOn is being built to ask every player for you, read their replies however they write them, and hand you one clear list: who is in, who is out and who needs a nudge.

Free, volunteer-built. Your email is used only to contact you about the beta.

Messy replies, sorted

Players answer in their own words. You get one tidy list instead of a long thread.

"yesss count me in 🎾"In
"ugh can't, kid has a recital"Out
"I can play but have to leave by 4"In, until 4:00
"maybe?? depends on work"Not sure: asks a follow-up

How it works

  1. 1Add your teamShare your roster and schedule once.
  2. 2The agent asksEach player gets an email before the match.
  3. 3Players just replyNo app to download and no form to learn.
  4. 4You get the listSee who can play and who still needs an answer.

Then, the extras

  • Lineup ideasOptions that follow ALTA's movement and sandbagging rules, with a reason for every line.Planned for your league. Today the lineup rules cover ALTA only.
  • Rules answers with sourcesAsk a question and see the ALTA rulebook section behind the answer.Planned for your league's rules. Today this covers ALTA only.

You stay in charge

Every lineup and every message to the whole team needs the captain's approval. You publish the lineup on ALTA yourself; the agent never logs in. WhosOn is still in testing, and beta captains will help shape what it does.
