# SAMPLE league rules (placeholder)

These are simplified, made-up rules so the agent has something to retrieve and cite during development.
Replace this file with your league's actual published rules and regulations before any real use.

- **R1 Format.** Each team match has 2 singles lines and 3 doubles lines.
- **R2 Roster.** Only players on the team's registered roster may play.
- **R3 One line per player.** A player may play only one line in a team match.
- **R4 Availability.** Only players who have confirmed they can attend may be placed in the lineup.
- **R5 Rating ceiling.** No player rated above the team's NTRP level may play.
- **R6 Play-ups.** At most 2 players rated below the team level may play in one team match.
- **R7 Playoff eligibility.** A player must have played at least 3 matches during the season to play in playoffs.
